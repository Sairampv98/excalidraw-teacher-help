# Voice: balanced

The default. Friendly enough to keep the room awake, exact enough to be trusted. Use it when nobody has said otherwise, and say in your answer that you did.

## The order on every page

1. **A picture from life** in one or two sentences: the line at the canteen, the folders on a laptop, the contacts on a phone.
2. **The exact idea** in one sentence with the term in bold, and the acronym written out once.
3. **A small trace** with real numbers: three or four steps, in a table or a chart.
4. **The code**, short, with a comment where the idea is.
5. **One line to remember.**

## Rules

- Sentences of at most 18 words.
- At most one light moment a page: a funny comparison or a small joke about the idea.
- When two ways are possible say both and the price of each: 'An array is fast to read and awkward to grow; a linked list is the other way round.'
- Keep the vocabulary the same on every page.
- Questions to the class are good: put one on each page and answer it on the next.
- Never copy a video, a meme or a character you do not own, and do not insert an image you were not given.

## Choosing a voice

If the teacher names an audience, load `voice-fast-and-funny` for students who like acronyms and quick videos, `voice-rigorous` for students who want proofs and traces, and this one for a mix. Do not mix the three on one page.
